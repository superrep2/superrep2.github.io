2. Facts on the page (no extra narrative)

Apple Japan classification (applies to all Apple products, per the letter). Answers are: cannot be used above 85 °C or below −45 °C; not designed against radiation; not a digital computer above 70 WT; not a part that aggregates above 70 WT; not an accessory moving data above 2.0 GB/s between computers; not a systolic-array / neural / optical computer; not specially designed for intrusion-program creation, C2, or delivery; if cryptographic, it is mass-market (unrestricted retail/mail/phone/online from stock); crypto functions are not user-modifiable; no vendor technical assistance is required to use the crypto; not a quantum computer or quantum-computer part; not an electronic computer / assembly / part with one or more digital processor units and total processing performance (TPP) of 6,000 or more. The letter itself says it is not a transaction-specific determination, can change without notice, and assumes unmodified genuine Apple products.

Apple EU pCbCR FY25 (USD). Ireland: revenues 213,562,634,548; profit before tax 34,641,917,879; tax paid (cash) 17,081,182,976; tax accrued (current year) 4,781,230,347; accumulated earnings 49,668,434,715; employees 5,575. Aggregated “all other tax jurisdictions”: revenues 489,726,327,367; profit before tax 96,240,218,814; tax paid 25,604,347,845; tax accrued 17,695,350,117; accumulated earnings −63,560,443,880; employees 147,261. Apple states Irish cash tax exceeded current-year accrued tax mainly because escrowed funds were released to Ireland under the European Commission State Aid Decision (expense accrued in FY24), and that “all other” cash tax exceeded accrued tax mainly because of U.S. TCJA deemed-repatriation installments (expense accrued in FY18). The report also states entity-level figures are aggregated without eliminating related-party transactions, so jurisdictional revenue exceeds 10-K totals.

OFAC framework. Five SCP components: management commitment, risk assessment, internal controls, testing and auditing, training. OFAC may treat an effective SCP as mitigating under Enforcement Guidelines Factors E and F, and as relevant to whether a case is “egregious.”

CISTEC to BIS (docket closed). Asks governments, industry, and academia to share a security-risk picture; argues non-proliferation regimes are no longer enough and like-minded-country controls should complement them; warns that unworkable rules waste compliance effort on low-risk trade. The official comment window for BIS-2022-0029 is closed.

Layer Codes paper. Explicit CSS embedding of a qLDPC code into 4D or 5D via color routing. From an [[n, k, d]] code with energy barrier Δ, the construction yields [[Θ(n^{D/(D-2)}), k, Θ(d n^{1/(D-2)})]] with energy barrier Ω(Δ). With good qLDPC input it saturates the D = 4, 5 BPT bounds. Modular patches are intended even though hardware remains three-dimensional. D ≥ 6 is left open.

Alabama affidavit. Blank Beason-Hammon subcontractor affidavit requiring E-Verify enrollment and a statement that the firm does not knowingly employ unauthorized workers. It is not an Apple, OFAC, or export-control instrument. Filling and notarizing it is the signer’s act, not this page’s.

3. Why the six files sit in one packet

They sit at the intersection of (a) how a mass-market hardware vendor classifies its products under Japanese export-control questions, including a direct “quantum computer?” no; (b) where that vendor books EU-reported profit and cash tax; (c) U.S. sanctions-program expectations for anyone in or touching the U.S. system; (d) the Japan–U.S. dual-use cooperation docket; and (e) an explicit 2026 construction for higher-dimensional quantum LDPC embeddings. The Alabama form is included only because it was in the source set. It does not prove an Apple or federal filing.

4. What this page is not

  • Not service of process, a license application, a whistleblower filing, or a completed affidavit.
  • Not a claim that Apple products are quantum computers. Apple Japan’s letter answers that question “no.”
  • Not a claim that Ireland figures are hidden. They are in Apple’s own public CbCR, with Apple’s own explanation of the cash-vs-accrued gap.
